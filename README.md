# TerraGuard GIS

An intelligent geoinformation system for monitoring, preserving, and restoring fertile topsoil during construction. Developed as a diploma project.

## Problem statement

Construction can damage or remove fertile topsoil. Project teams need a way to locate affected areas, record soil conditions, and track preservation and restoration activities. TerraGuard GIS aims to bring this information together in a map-based system.

## Target users

Intended users include environmental specialists, construction project managers, and land reclamation specialists. Their needs and workflows will be validated through user research.

## Goal and expected outcomes

Support soil protection throughout construction by combining geospatial records, soil condition monitoring, and restoration planning.

Expected deliverables:

- A map-based prototype for viewing construction sites and topsoil protection areas.
- Records of soil observations and preservation activities.
- Tools for planning and tracking topsoil removal, storage, reuse, and restoration.
- An evaluation of the prototype against defined requirements and a baseline workflow.

## Scope

The proposed initial scope covers site mapping, soil observation records, and tracking preservation and restoration activities. Intelligent analysis methods, required datasets, and evaluation metrics will be defined during research and requirements analysis.

Automated machinery control, dedicated sensor hardware, and certification of regulatory compliance are outside the proposed initial scope. Final scope remains subject to requirements validation and supervisor review.
